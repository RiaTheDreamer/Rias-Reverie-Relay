# Included Surfaces

Reverie Relay includes 46 configurable Surface Utilities across messaging, social, narrative, photography, editorial, and promotional contexts. They are selectable individually from **Surfaces → Library**.

Examples include Smartphone, KakaoTalk, iMessage, Discord, Instagram, X, Email Thread, Phone Gallery, Photo Booth Strip, Polaroid, Character Profile, Relationship Map, Diary, Notes, Naver News, Newspaper, Case File, Court Transcript, Medical Record, Album Cover, Magazine Cover, Music Player, TikTok, Twitch, YouTube, and Voice Memo.

The available set is shown directly in the extension, including each Utility's short explanation and injected-prompt toggle.
