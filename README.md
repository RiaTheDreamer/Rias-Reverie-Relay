# Reverie Relay

**For stories you can get a little more lost in. ♡**

A phone buzzing at exactly the wrong moment. An Instagram profile full of questionable life choices. A diary entry that really should have stayed private. A picture of the scene you just spent twenty minutes emotionally destroying yourself over.

Reverie brings those little pieces of the world into your **Lumiverse** chats: interactive apps, documents, narrative extras, illustrations and a persistent in-world phone. You choose what belongs in your story and how much of it you want.

I like regex. I like pretty things. And if something looks like it has a button, I am going to want to press it. This is what happened when nobody stopped me. <3

## Little windows into your world

Reverie calls these **Surfaces**: story-generated content presented as something you can open, read, browse or interact with.

- **Messages & social apps:** texts, Discord chats, Instagram profiles and DMs, Reddit threads, Tinder profiles, emails and more.
- **Personal things:** diaries, notes, photo galleries, polaroids and voice memos.
- **A world doing its own thing:** news articles, case files, records, album covers, magazines and music players.
- **Narrative extras:** Plot Sparks, Parallel Scenes, character introductions, dossiers and location files.

The model supplies the content; Reverie gives it a home. Open a conversation, switch tabs, browse profiles or look through an album. The available interactions depend on the Surface.

Enable your favorites, save selections as presets, and choose whether they appear directly in the message or behind a button. There are sparkling and glass options, obviously. I have priorities.

## Pictures where the story happens

Illustrations sit alongside the moments they depict, with two ways to plan them:

- **Relay Planned:** Reverie reads the response and plans illustrated moments from the scene.
- **Story Model Planned:** your story model writes image requests into its response, choosing where each illustration belongs.

Choose Natural Language or Booru Tag prompts, your image count and a framing style. Scene Snapshot, Sequence, Emotional Beat, character-focused views, Persona POV, Storyboard and Blocking Fidelity give you different ways to approach a scene. **Scene Flow** can choose framing separately for each image; **Dreamcut** focuses on source-grounded details and cutaways.

You can also add a designed title cover with its own artwork, use **Sightline Guard** to help keep an action visible in the chosen crop, or try **Quickweave** for compact Relay planning.

Each image has its own status card and controls. Inspect or edit its prompt, reparse, regenerate, open the lightbox or revisit earlier versions in image history. You get to be picky. It's your story.

## Let them keep their face. And change their clothes.

**Appearance Memory** keeps stable character details separate from current outfits and temporary scene changes. Edit the saved sheets, remove unwanted traits, choose how often full updates happen, or trigger a rescan yourself. Explicit outfit changes can inform the current scene between scheduled scans.

Optional **Portrait Insight** can use a compatible vision connection to fill missing appearance details from character avatars.

The goal is visual continuity. The image model still has a vote, unfortunately: anatomy, exact contact and prompt adherence depend on your model, settings and LoRAs.

## Yes, they have phones

**Reverie Phone** gives your persona and characters persistent phones, including members of group chats. Read incoming messages, browse apps, reply in conversations and bring phone events back into the main story.

When a Core app Surface appears in a saved story response, it syncs to its matching phone app. That Reddit thread, diary or dating profile can be opened and interacted with there, using the content the story model actually produced. Narrative extras stay in the chat.

You can also set up app content directly inside the phone. Conversations and app records persist, and supported apps have their own messaging or comment controls. **Use in Story** can place delivered texts in your composer for you to edit; phone context can also be included in the next outgoing story prompt.

These are fictional apps inside your world. Their posts and conversations stay within Lumiverse.

## Make it yours

Pick the Surfaces you want, their presentation and colors, illustration behavior, Appearance update frequency and phone preferences. You can create custom Surfaces, add image support to your own trackers, and inspect the exact instructions Reverie contributes to the story prompt.

Start with a few things you love. You can always enable the entire buffet later. I understand the temptation, believe me.

Reverie uses your configured Lumiverse connections. Illustrations need a compatible image-generation connection; planning and phone replies use the relevant text-model connections. Provider usage and costs apply.
